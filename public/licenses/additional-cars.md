# Additional car models

The bundled `models/brz.glb` and `models/m3.glb` are adapted from the Stunt Rally sources below. Conversion retains the final BRZ body, glass, interior and wheel meshes with their runtime texture atlases. The BMW retains the CC0 body, glass and wheel geometry, with newly generated cabin, trim, wing and lamps and our own untextured PBR materials. Both conversions remove working duplicates and source animation, normalize coordinates/pivots, and repeat the authored wheel assembly at four hubs. These changes were made for this game on 26 October 2026. The models and simulator tuning do not imply manufacturer endorsement.

## BMW M3 E92 race car (`m3.glb`)

Original model by **Neubi**, from Blend Swap. Upstream states **CC Zero** ([CC0 text](CC0.txt), [online](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/)).

Original link preserved from upstream: http://www.blendswap.com/blends/view/19869

The upstream notice additionally credits import, settings and interior to **CryHam**. This conversion excludes the separately attributed `XZ_interior` mesh and all XZ texture atlases. The replacement cabin, dashboard, bucket seats, steering wheel, gauges, shifter, trim, wing and lamp geometry/materials are generated by this game's conversion script and are released under CC0. The surviving original Neubi body, glass and wheel mesh geometry uses the original model's CC0 designation.

Reason for replacing the atlases: upstream [docs/data.md](https://github.com/stuntrally/stuntrally3/blob/fe3ecd73fff671c3d75ecaeadccea5c5e6afe0d8/docs/data.md) explicitly identifies uncertain photographic sources for the old XZ rear-light and underside textures. None of these textures, nor any other XZ source texture, is embedded in the shipped GLB. No new license is assigned to CryHam's excluded cabin or uncertain photographic textures. The generated cabin is a game approximation, not a factory interior reconstruction.
