APEX / Credits & sources

A browser driving experience. Alpine Pass is an original fictional route and procedural environment. Nordschleife uses real-world road geometry; scenery is approximate. Vehicle tuning is fictional and is not manufacturer calibrated.

Vehicle

Car Concept © 2024 Eric Chadwick, Darmstadt Graphics Group GmbH, CC BY 4.0. Adaptations: scaled/reoriented model, adjustable paint, animated wheel hierarchy, brake emission, cockpit camera. Original source notes. Included Khronos and 3D Commerce marks remain subject to Khronos trademark terms; their inclusion does not imply endorsement.

Additional cars: Subaru BRZ Varis by Socksthecat (CC BY 4.0), and BMW M3 race-car original model by Neubi (CC0), adapted for this game. Model sources, authors, adaptations and notices.

Simulation and circuit

Vehicle solver, track queries, base world/terrain and engine worklet adapted from Nürburgring Drive © 2026 bwchoi, MIT. Modifications include local PBR textures, foliage cards, mountain terrain, analog control/session integration and a new rendering/UI layer.

Nordschleife track data © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL 1.0. The redistributed derived database is available here under ODbL 1.0. Elevation from open-elevation.com / SRTM, public domain. The new Alpine Pass route is fictional and is not derived from OpenStreetMap.

Materials and lighting

Asphalt021, Grass004 and Ground037 textures by Lennart Demes / ambientCG, CC0 1.0, retrieved from the FPS Buildings Environment Kit mirror. Optimized JPEGs retain 2K resolution. Mirror provenance.

Venice Sunset HDR environment by Poly Haven (CC0), distributed with the Three.js examples. Sky and sun use Three.js Sky. Foliage silhouettes and contact shadow textures are generated by this project.

Nearby 3D trees generated with EZ-Tree © 2024 Daniel Greenheck, MIT. Leaf textures from its npm 1.1.0 release, verified against the upstream Git LFS SHA-256 identifiers. Generator source pinned at dcf309bd86bd521083d9c70f01f2de45fdc7c457; local modifications normalize scale and reduce mesh detail. Distant foliage silhouettes remain original generated cards.

Audio and libraries

BRZ boxer-four and BMW race V8 banks by CryHam (CC BY 4.0), recorded from engine-sim, with separate RPM samples. Additional engine sources and modifications.

Default V8 engine: CryHam / Stunt Rally 3 V8 sample bank © 2024 CryHam, CC BY 4.0. Samples were recorded from engine-sim, not a real production car; APEX adds loop crossfades, normalization, Ogg encoding, RPM blending and load/cockpit filtering. Source and adaptation details.

Fallback engine DSP adapted from Antonio-R1's Engine Sound Generator © 2021–2022 Antonio-R1, MIT, through bwchoi's worklet port. Tires, wind, shifts, road noise and the fallback engine are synthesized; no Sonniss recordings are bundled.

Three.js © Three.js authors, MIT. Vite © Vite contributors, MIT. Full source license notices are included in the repository and dependency distributions.
