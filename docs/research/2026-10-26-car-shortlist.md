## Additional candidate, pending rights verification

Downloaded and parsed: 1,681,572 bytes, 58 nodes, named four wheel assemblies, interior and steering wheel. Draco decoder is required by the Three.js example. The asset itself contains no license metadata and the original Sketchfab page is inaccessible in this environment. Do not infer asset rights from Three.js's software MIT license. Keep as a backup pending original-model license verification.

## What makes these different in our game

The existing solver supports FWD or RWD, not true AWD. Add center/front/rear torque distribution and test it before offering AWD cars. Engine sound banks need different cylinder character and load response; changing playback pitch alone is insufficient. The existing engine-sim source includes flat-four and V12 sample families, but their suitability for a specific model still needs auditioning. These are simulator-generated sounds, not recordings of these actual cars.

Each vehicle needs explicit mass, inertia, weight distribution, wheelbase, track widths/radii, torque/RPM curve, gearing, differential, suspension, brake balance, tire characteristics and aero. Tune these against identified reference measurements. Current fictional tuning or upstream Stunt Rally profiles are not manufacturer calibration.

Import only the selected car at runtime; convert sources to GLB, rebuild PBR materials with local textures, normalize pivots/scale, bind wheels and cockpit independently, and provide lower-detail remote cars for multiplayer. Inspect cockpit quality in the target renderer before promising parity with the APEX interior. Imported source files and previews currently live in `/tmp/apex-sports-candidates`, outside the shipped assets.
